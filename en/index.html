FAQ

Questions

Why are you cheaper than agencies?

I work solo, without managers or an office. You pay for the work, not overhead. In return I ask for an honest review.

What if I don't like the result?

That's why there is a free demo first. If it doesn't click, we part ways with no payment. After that, edits are included.

Will I own the code and accounts?

Yes. Domain, hosting, bot and code are registered to you; I hand everything over after payment.

Do you work with other countries?

Yes: Belarus, Russia, Kazakhstan and beyond. We talk on Telegram and agree on a payment method.

Where do I track my order and pay?

Everything is in my bot @KirdunSupportBot: a numbered request, its status, questions and the invoice. Pay with Telegram Stars or by bank transfer; I send details after we agree.

How fast do you reply?

Usually within a couple of hours in the daytime (Minsk, UTC+3). At night the bot takes your request and shows an estimate right away, and I reply in the morning.

What do you need from me to start?

A couple of sentences about the task and examples you like. I'll ask the rest.
